## Retrieval guidance

- Queries about **IC identification, TTL/CMOS, logic gates, floating inputs, fan-out, or ESD** should point to Chapter 1, especially printed pages 1–4 and 28–36.
- Queries about **logic equations or simplifying a gate network** should point to Chapters 2–3.
- Queries about **latches, flip-flops, state machines, counters, and shift registers** should point to Chapters 4–6 and 8–9.
- Queries about **multiplexers, encoders, decoders, or seven-segment displays** should point to Chapter 7.
- Queries about **555 timing circuits, oscillation, or PWM fundamentals** should point to Chapter 10.
- Queries about **simulation of basic digital circuits** should point to Chapter 11.

## Safety and evidence boundaries

1. Treat the book as a foundational educational reference only.
2. Do not assume its example component values, logic-family voltage ranges, or timing characteristics apply to a different IC family, manufacturer, or current device revision.
3. Do not infer a fault cause from a general digital-electronics explanation alone.
4. Do not instruct live probing, bypassing interlocks, defeating protections, or handling energized control boards based only on this book.
5. Before intervention, follow site isolation/LOTO rules and the exact manufacturer's safety instructions; electrical measurements and board-level work require qualified personnel.
6. Preserve uncertainty: cite the book and printed page as educational background, while keeping device-specific claims separate and requiring exact datasheet/manual evidence.
